# STORY LINE

Sometimes life doesn't go as planned, but every setback is a chance to bounce back. <br/>

Step into Alex's world, explore his apartment, interact with the environment, and experience his story through conversations and small discoveries. <br/>

BOUNCE BACK is more than just a game theme — it's a reminder that no matter what happens, you can always get back up and try again<br/>






# Why we made this ?
We wanted to create a game around something everyone can relate to: life doesn't always go according to plan. <br/>

Sometimes things get messy, unexpected problems come up, and everything feels a little overwhelming. But every setback is also a chance to learn, make things better, and try again.<br/>

That's what BOUNCE BACK means to us. You don't have to have everything figured out. You just have to keep moving forward.
